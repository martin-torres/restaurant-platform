# Restaurant Platform

## Product Purpose
A mobile-first restaurant ordering and management platform that connects diners with restaurants. Customers browse menus, place orders, track delivery, and pay — all from their phone. Staff manage orders through a live dashboard. Owners get real-time business intelligence.

## Users

**Diners** (primary, largest group)
- Hungry people at a table or picking up to-go
- Want speed, clarity, and payment flexibility
- On mobile, in a restaurant or at home
- Mood: casual to focused — order food, pay, leave

**Restaurant Staff**
- Servers (FOH) who manage tables, orders, and service flow
- Kitchen staff (BOH) who see tickets and mark items ready
- Bartenders who see drink orders in a separate view
- Managers who oversee operations, comp items, close shifts

**Restaurant Owners**
- Business owners monitoring multiple KPIs
- Need occupancy, revenue, popular items, staff performance
- Desktop-first: data-dense, actionable

## Tone
Warm, confident, and capable. Not cute, not cold. The brand feels like a well-run restaurant — polished but not pretentious, friendly but efficient.

## Register
product — design serves the product. Every decision improves speed, clarity, or satisfaction for a specific user task.

## Anti-references
- Over-designed food blogs with giant hero photos and decorative typography
- Dark SaaS apps pretending to be "enterprise" — no fake complexity
- QR-code menus that are just PDFs — this is interactive, not a brochure
- Restaurant apps that hide the menu behind an account wall
